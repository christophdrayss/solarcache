How does cache sharing actually work? ▾
  1. Hash — your box hashes the model ID + quantization + prefix tokens.
  2. Match — the network index returns peers holding that cache.
  3. Request — your box pulls the KV cache from the nearest peer.
  4. Verify — cryptographic check ensures bit-exact reproduction.
  5. Pay — micropayment routes back to the originating seeder.
  6. Use — inference resumes with prefill cost ~0.

Questions people ask.

Does it work without solar?

Yes. Off-peak grid pricing works too — anywhere with a meaningful spot/peak delta.

Which models?

Any llama.cpp compatible model, 8B–34B. Quantizations Q4–Q8.

What if I update my model?

Cache rebuild is scheduled automatically at the next solar peak.

Is this a mining rig?

No. It creates no tokens for sale. It reduces your inference cost — and pays you when others reuse what you've already computed.

How does the marketplace work?

Your box fingerprints every prefill and uploads the cache to the SolarCache index. When another node needs the same prefix, they pay a micro-fee. You earn passively.

What contexts get shared?

Only explicitly public or opted-in contexts. Your private queries never leave your box.

Who sets the price per cache hit?

Dynamic pricing based on cache size, token count, and network demand. Real-time earnings visible in your dashboard.

What stops someone from stealing my cache?

Cache hits are cryptographically verified. You get paid per use, not per download.
